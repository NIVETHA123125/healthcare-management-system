FACILITY OVERVIEW

State of the Art Outpatient Care

Founded in 2024, CareGrid is a progressive, community-oriented general practice clinic built with modern diagnostic architectures. We provide flat-rate care structures, making family consultations accessible, swift, and completely clear of insurance ambiguity.

Our facility includes twelve general consultation suites, three specialized triage units, and a dedicated pediatric diagnostic wing staffed by certified specialists.

MISSION & VISION

Our Mission

To provide highly efficient, flat-rate, and accessible outpatient primary care through modern clinical interfaces, minimizing administrative barriers for both practitioners and patients.
